# Mission Statement

## Mission

The mission of the Eternal Existence Archives (EEA) is to preserve humanity’s collective knowledge, culture, and achievements while safeguarding the long-term survival of human civilization. By archiving documents, digital media, and cultural artifacts in multiple formats in redundant secure locations, we ensure that humanity can endure and learn from past natural, global, and societal events.

EEA serves as a proactive safeguard against the repetition of history’s destructive cycles. By maintaining a comprehensive, accessible record of human experience, we empower future generations to make informed decisions, mitigate preventable risks, and protect the continuity of life and civilization. Our work is guided by principles of accuracy, inclusivity, and non-partisanship, creating a reliable and enduring resource for scholars, educators, and the public.
