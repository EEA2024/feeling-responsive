## Legal Protection, Founding Origin & Funding

The Eternal Existence Archives (EEA) was conceived and founded by Stephen A Taritas, as a global initiative dedicated to preserving humanity’s knowledge, culture, and achievements. All original ideas, documents, frameworks, processes, products, concepts, and physical or digital mediums created under EEA originate from this founding development and are protected as intellectual property.

Contributors, whether individuals, collaborative groups, boards, or organizational partners, retain authorship of their original works but grant EEA the right to archive, preserve, and use these contributions in alignment with its mission. All materials, concepts, processes, products, and derived works within the archive are safeguarded to ensure the integrity, continuity, and protective purpose of EEA — including the preservation of human knowledge and civilization against loss, misuse, or disruption.

Any profits or revenue generated through activities, services, or products associated with EEA are dedicated solely to supporting the initiative, funding its ongoing development, expansion, and protective mission. This framework ensures that EEA, under its founder’s guidance, maintains legal stewardship over the collective body of knowledge, technologies, and methodologies, while recognizing and respecting the rights of contributors in any form. Participation in the initiative implies acknowledgment of these protections and a shared commitment to the responsible use and long-term safeguarding of humanity’s intellectual and cultural legacy.
