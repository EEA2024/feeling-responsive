# Eternal Existence Archives (EEA)

The Eternal Existence Archives (EEA) is a global initiative dedicated to preserving the full legacy of humanity — including intellectual, cultural, historical, and technological achievements, both celebrated and cautionary. Our mission is to safeguard humanity’s knowledge and heritage against unforeseen disruptions, ensuring that the lessons and accomplishments of the past remain accessible and secure for current and future generations.

## Our Mission

EEA serves as a resilient repository for human knowledge. By archiving documents, digital media, and cultural artifacts in multiple formats and locations, we ensure continuity, prevent loss, and maintain a reliable resource for generations to come.

## Core Principles

- **Continuity:** Preserve knowledge across time and formats.  
- **Resilience:** Maintain archives across secure, distributed locations and in multiple formats.  
- **Accessibility:** Ensure information is retrievable and used responsibly by future generations.  
- **Non-partisanship:** Focus on the human legacy, not ideology.  
- **Proactive Protection:** Learn from history to mitigate avoidable cycles and disasters — manmade, natural, or cosmic.

## How to Contribute

EEA invites scholars, archivists, and all of humanity to support its mission by contributing knowledge, resources, and expertise. Collaborative efforts strengthen the integrity and reach of the archive.
