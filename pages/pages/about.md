## About the Eternal Existence Archives (EEA)

The Eternal Existence Archives (EEA) exists to preserve the collective memory of humanity. Our work encompasses all aspects of human achievement — from cultural and historical, natural to scientific, technological, and social knowledge. By capturing and safeguarding this information, we create a permanent record that can guide future generations, helping them learn from the past and build a more informed existence.

EEA is committed to protecting humanity’s legacy against loss or disruption. Our approach emphasizes multiple formats, distributed storage, and long-term accessibility. We focus on accuracy, inclusivity, and non-partisanship, ensuring that the archive remains a reliable resource for scholars, educators, and the public.

Through collaboration and contribution, EEA invites everyone to participate in preserving humanity’s knowledge — because the lessons, events, and losses of the past are only valuable if they are remembered.
